What I do
======

My work focuses on mathematical systems and control theory. In particular, I investigate the following topics.

------
- **Data-driven control:** In this line of research, we focus on system identification/analysis and control synthesis using data and prior knowledge. See our recent work, "[Data-driven stabilization using prior knowledge on stabilizability and controllability](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.25452)."

------
- **Experiment design:** In this line of research, we focus on finding inputs that force a system to generate informative data. For this goal, we leverage prior knowledge of the system. See our recent work, "[Experiment design for set-membership identification: From prior knowledge to universal inputs](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.00844)."

------
- **Robust control:** Here, we investigate fundamental limitations and possibilities in robust stabilization. See our recent work "[Robust stabilization of discrete-time linear systems requires nonlinear dynamic feedback](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.19010)."
